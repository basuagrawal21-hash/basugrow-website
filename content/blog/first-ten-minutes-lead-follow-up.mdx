We can build you a campaign that produces forty enquiries a month at a good price. If those enquiries sit in an inbox until evening, you will convert a fraction of them, and you will conclude that ads do not work for your business.

This is the single most common way a perfectly good account fails.

## What actually happens to a lead

Someone is on Instagram at 2pm. They see your ad, think "I have been meaning to sort this out", and fill in a form. For about ten minutes, you have their full attention.

Then the moment passes. They go back to work. By evening they have half-forgotten which gym they enquired at. By tomorrow, if a competitor called them at 2:15pm, they have already booked with someone else.

Nothing about the lead changed. Only the timing did.

## Why speed beats almost everything else

Consider two businesses running identical campaigns at the same cost per enquiry.

The first checks leads once a day and calls in the evening. Perhaps a third pick up; the rest have moved on. Say one in ten of the answered calls books.

The second replies within minutes, while the person still has the phone in their hand. Two-thirds engage, and closer to a third book.

Same ads, same spend, several times the customers. No targeting change we could make would come close to that.

## Why it goes wrong in local businesses

It is almost never laziness. It is that the owner is on the gym floor, in a treatment room, or with a walk-in customer. Leads arrive by email to an address nobody has open. Nobody is ignoring them; nobody has seen them.

So the fix is not "try harder". It is removing the gap between the enquiry and someone knowing about it.

## What to put in place

1. **Send leads to WhatsApp, not email.** A notification on the phone that is already in your pocket. This one change does most of the work.
2. **Send an automatic first message.** Something that confirms what they asked for and says when you will call. It holds the moment while you finish what you are doing.
3. **Decide who calls.** One named person per shift. "Someone will get to it" means nobody does.
4. **Give it a window.** Within the hour during working hours. Not aspirational — actually agreed.
5. **Write down what to say.** A short script for the first call, one follow-up message if they do not pick up, and a point at which you stop.
6. **Track the gap.** How long between an enquiry arriving and someone responding. Watch it the way you watch cost per lead.

## The uncomfortable part

If you cannot answer enquiries within a few hours, more leads will not help you. They will cost you money and leave you with a worse opinion of advertising.
